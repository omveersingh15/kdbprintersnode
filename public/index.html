Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our printing services

What file formats do you accept for printing?

We prefer PDF files for the best print accuracy. However, we also accept Word documents (DOCX), high-resolution images (JPG, PNG), and design files (CDR, AI, PSD). For Thesis/Dissertations, PDF is mandatory to ensure formatting stays intact.

How long does it take to print and bind a thesis?

For standard urgent orders, we can complete printing and hard binding within 24 hours. Normal delivery takes 2-3 days. Please call us for express service availability depending on our current workload.

Do you offer delivery services outside Ranchi?

Yes, we ship printed materials across India via reliable courier partners. Shipping charges apply based on weight and location. Local delivery within Ranchi is also available.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No, there is no minimum order quantity for digital printing. You can print a single copy of a book or report. However, offset printing for large volumes (500+ copies) offers significantly lower cost per unit.
